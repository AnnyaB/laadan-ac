# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This repository is intended to support open, respectful, and reproducible research discussion around LAADAN-AC and admissibility-aware offline treatment-policy learning. Contributors, users, and readers are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of expected behaviour include:

* using welcoming and respectful language;
* giving constructive feedback;
* focusing discussion on the code, experiments, documentation, and reproducibility;
* respecting different levels of background knowledge;
* reporting issues clearly and in good faith.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

* harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language;
* insulting or dismissive comments about contributors or users;
* deliberately misleading issue reports or pull requests;
* sharing private or sensitive information without permission;
* using the repository to promote unsafe clinical use.

## Research and clinical-use note

LAADAN-AC is a benchmark research implementation. It is not a clinical decision-support system and must not be used to guide patient treatment. Discussions and contributions should respect this boundary.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove comments, issues, or pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may lead to a user being blocked from participating in the repository.

## Contact

For concerns about conduct in this repository, please contact the repository maintainer through GitHub.
